<h2>Brazilian institutions reference</h2>

<p>
<a href="https://github.com/hsigstad/brazil-institutions">brazil-institutions</a>
is a dense, citation-backed reference on Brazilian legal and political
institutions — courts, procedure, the Ministério Público, fiscal federalism,
electoral system, procurement, corruption statutes, labor justice, and related
topics. Designed to be read by both researchers and LLM agents.
</p>

<p>
It includes 36 topical files with inline statute and jurisprudence citations,
a citation resolver (<code>cite.py</code>) that looks up any statute article
or court case from a compact backtick citation, annotated legislation
databases (STF decisions mapped to each CF article; TSE jurisprudence mapped
to each Electoral Code article), and indices of STF, TSE, TST, and STJ
súmulas. Released under CC BY 4.0.
</p>
