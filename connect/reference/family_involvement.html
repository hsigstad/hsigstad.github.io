    Family Involvement in Judicial Corruption Episodes

    Classification of all documented judicial corruption episodes by whether relatives of judges played a role in the scheme.

    Methodology note

    Our news collection (~596 articles across 30+ outlets) used two types of search queries:

    1. Corruption-focused queries — venda sentencas, venda decisoes judiciais, corrupção judiciário, operation-specific names. These find judicial corruption regardless of whether family members are involved.
    2. Family-focused queries — filhotismo, parente desembargador advogado, filho advogado ministro, Viviane Barci, etc. These specifically search for family connections.

    For a valid claim about the share of corruption scandals involving family members, we restrict to episodes discoverable through corruption-focused searches (Group A). Episodes found only through family-focused searches (Group B) are reported separately — including them would introduce selection bias (selecting on the outcome).

    See references/news/README.md for the complete search log with all queries, outlets, and page depths.

    Combined summary (for paper text)

    Conservative claim (Group A only, independent operations):

    We systematically collected all documented episodes of judicial corruption (venda de sentenças) in Brazil since 2008 through corruption-focused searches across ~596 news articles from 30+ outlets. We identified 12 independent operations. Of these, 10 (83%) involved relatives of judges in operational roles — as bribe collectors, money launderers, decision brokers, or financial conduits. Among the 9 cases where relatives' professions are known, 8 (89%) involved relatives who were practicing lawyers. The legal profession provides professional cover, a legitimate reason for large payments ("honorários"), and plausible deniability.

    Taxonomy of family roles

    Across all episodes (Groups A and B), relatives served these functions:

    Role Episodes
    Bribe collection/delivery Faroeste, Mais Valia, 18 Minutos, Fortaleza
    Money laundering (shell companies, fake transactions, asset concealment) Faroeste, Última Ratio, SP, MT
    Financial conduit (law firm fees, contracts, fractionated deposits) Última Ratio, Sisamnes, Mais Valia, Banco Master
    Decision brokering (negotiating sales of decisions) Faroeste, Naufrágio, Churrascada
    Cross-family cooperation (children of different judges collaborating) Faroeste, Última Ratio
    Influence peddling (leveraging family name without formal role) Sisamnes, Emmanoel/TST, Fux
    Process manipulation (rigging case distribution) Naufrágio
    Contract insertion (placed into lucrative contracts as payment) MT/Sisamnes
    Obstruction (destroying evidence, warning judges) Faroeste
