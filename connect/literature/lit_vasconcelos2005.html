    Vasconcelos (2005) --- Juizes no banco dos reus

    Book by Frederico Vasconcelos (Publifolha). Chronicles judicial corruption at the TRF3 (Federal Regional Court, 3rd Region, Sao Paulo) from the 1990s through 2003.

    Key cases

    Haddad and Theotonio Costa (Chapters 1--4)

    • Roberto Luiz Ribeiro Haddad and Paulo Theotonio Costa, desembargadores of the Primeira Turma TRF3, exposed in July 1999 Folha de Sao Paulo investigation for assets incompatible with judicial salaries.
    • Haddad: 33 vehicles (R$500k), yacht, superlanche, 8 chacaras, Sitio Sherwood, 3 sobrados. Promoted to desembargador by FHC in 1995.
    • Theotonio: partner in real estate firm, 7 buildings, farm in MS, multiple apartments/flats. Desembargador por merecimento since 1993.
    • Haddad falsified income-tax document with a fake stamp (carimbo). STJ received unanimously the denuncia against Haddad and his ex-wife for falsification (April 2003); Haddad afastado.
    • Theotonio manipulated distribution of habeas corpus petitions to benefit drug traffickers and politicians (Major Sergio Roberto de Carvalho, Orestes Quercia). Denounced for manipulating distribution in the case of Grupo Bamerindus (R$150 million in tutela antecipada). Intermediary Ismael Medeiros received R$1.5 million from Bamerindus, of which at least R$1.04 million was transferred as "loans" to Theotonio's companies (Thema, Kroonna).
    • Antonio Carlos Suplicy: perito judicial with a fake engineering diploma, appointed by Haddad (then titular judge of 15a Vara Federal). Overvalued a property at 10x its real worth. The Primeira Turma upheld Suplicy's inflated appraisals even after criminal charges for the fake diploma. Suplicy eventually sentenced to 3 years by judge Rocha Mattos.

    Rocha Mattos (Chapters 5, 7)

    • Joao Carlos da Rocha Mattos (RM): titular judge of the 4a Vara Federal. Concursado in 1984 (6th place). Former delegado (6 years) and procurador (2 years).
    • Known for controversial decisions benefiting high-profile criminals, politicians (Orestes Quercia), and businesspeople.
    • Lived in a luxury apartment owned by a Uruguayan offshore company; the company's procurador was advogado Carlos Alberto da Costa Silva, very active in cases before RM's vara.
    • Champion of correicoes: 60 representations and correicoes against him by procuradores. Afastado in 1992 for threatening TRF3 president Homar Cais; returned in 1996 via STJ liminar after prescription of the administrative process.
    • Threatened that he had information about "familiares de magistrados exercendo advocacia" and appeared to receive special treatment during plantoes.
    • Suffered from depression; ex-wife Norma Regina accused him of death threats in 2002.

    Casem Mazloum (Chapter 6)

    • Casem Mazloum: substitute of Rocha Mattos in the 4a Vara Federal, later juiz da 1a Vara Criminal Federal. Convicted of formacao de quadrilha in Anaconda.
    • Acquitted ex-senator Luiz Estevao and co-defendants in the Forum Trabalhista de SP scandal (R$169 million in alleged embezzlement), with controversial reasoning. In embargos de declaracao, reversed himself on admissibility of bank-secrecy evidence he himself had authorized.
    • Attempted to give Lalau (Nicolau dos Santos Neto) privileged prison conditions. Rejected tax-evasion denuncia against Lalau.

    Adriana Soveral

    • Adriana Soveral: juiza in cases involving Paulo Maluf. 67 representations and correicoes against her. Afastada by Orgao Especial in 2004.
    • Together with RM, used reserved PF license plates on personal vehicles.
    • Convicted for formacao de quadrilha in Anaconda.

    Family connections documented

    • Haddad's wife (Maria Cristina): advogada. Administered the family business (oficina Reno-Car Renovadora de Automoveis, which LOMAN Art. 36 forbids judges from owning). Prepared a defense dossier for tax authorities and TRF3. Haddad claimed her earnings explained their wealth. She managed the equestrian center Manege Sherwood.
    • Haddad's brother (Carlos Alberto): ran the car repair shop where Haddad's vehicles were "reformed." The brothers redistributed company shares after investigations to give Haddad a minority stake.
    • Theotonio Costa's wife: advogada. Earned R$120k from a desapropriacao case. Her colleague, retired state procurador Genesio Gazda, "lent" R$290k to Theotonio's construction firm.
    • Theotonio's brother (irmao): received R$460k cash "loan" from Acidonio Ferreira da Silva, suspected laranja (front man) of Theotonio. Acidonio was a longtime friend of Theotonio's father; Theotonio had been his lawyer for 8 years.
    • Theotonio's primo (cousin): address used in Bamerindus contracts belonged to the cousin; the same address was occupied by Theotonio's chefe de gabinete.
    • Rocha Mattos' ex-wife (Norma Regina Cunha): "administradora dos ganhos da organizacao" --- managed proceeds, moved money abroad. R$550k in cash seized at her apartment. Denunciada in Anaconda.
    • Rocha Mattos' sister (Vera Cecilia Vieira de Moraes): ex-auditora da Receita Federal. Friend of Norma Regina.
    • Rocha Mattos' niece (Vera Cristina Vieira de Moraes): delegada da PF, suspected of aiding a company linked to the corruption ring. Together with her father (Carlos Augusto Vieira de Moraes), served as lawyer for doleiro Sandor Figueiredo in cases before RM's vara. Assumed her delegada position via judicial liminar.
    • Norma Regina to RM in intercepted call: "Paulo Maluf telefonou para mim e pediu para falar com Roberto Haddad, que nao precisa ele se preocupar de julgar isso agora."
    • Mazloum brothers: Casem (juiz federal, 1a Vara Criminal), Ali (juiz federal, 7a Vara Criminal), Saad and Nadim (promotores de justica). Ali gave Lalau's daughter a sentence of R$10k to Fome Zero in lieu of 2-year reclusao for R$376k in tax evasion. Ali afastado, denounced for formacao de quadrilha, threats, and abuse of power (later extinguished by STF). Haddad tried to get Ali appointed to replace Theotonio; Orgao Especial refused.
    • Scartezzini's wife: ex-desembargadora TRF3, later advogada defending tucanos Pedro Malan and Pedro Parente in improbidade case. Scartezzini (ex-president TRF3, then ministro STJ) nomeado by FHC.
    • Procurador Samir Haddad: seen dining with Theotonio at Restaurante Tuareg. Samir's ex-wife was Theotonio's chefe de gabinete.

    Operacao Anaconda (2003)

    • PF operation initiated in 2003, made public after two years of investigation.
    • Credit to a young generation of PF intelligence officers in Brasilia attempting to purge corruption.
    • Started in Alagoas after intelligence that Jorge Luiz Bezerra da Silva was trying to corrupt PF agents for privileged information.
    • Wiretapping was central. On the eve of the operation, intercepted calls showed instructions to destroy documents and remove computers from offices.
    • Wagner Rocha ("Peru"): had no law degree but acted as intermediary between beneficiaries and judges. Documents of Haddad found in his office. His office used for meetings coordinating deals on behalf of Casem, Adriana, and RM.
    • Denunciados: judges RM, Casem Mazloum, Ali Mazloum; policiais Bellini, Bertin, Cesar Herman Rodriguez, Bezerra; advogados Carlos Alberto Costa Silva, Affonso Passarelli Filho; empresarios Wagner Rocha, Sergio Chiamarelli; ex-auditora Norma Regina Cunha.
    • Subprocurador-geral Antonio Augusto Cesar was also part of the quadrilha but not included in the TRF3 denuncia (foro privilegiado at STJ). STJ Corte Especial received denuncia against Augusto Cesar and Cesar Herman for corruption in August 2004.
    • Sentencing: TRF3 convicted 10 of 11 for formacao de quadrilha; acquitted Bertin. RM, Cesar Herman, Bellini, Bezerra: 3 years reclusao + loss of position. Casem: 2 years reclusao + loss of position (converted to 100 salarios minimos). STF excluded Ali Mazloum via HC.
    • Defense attorneys filed 47 unsuccessful HC petitions at STF/STJ and 89 attempts to block TRF3 jurisdiction.
    • Of 8 varas federais in SP, 4 judges were removed.
    • Desembargadora Terezinha Cazerta: relatora. Procuradoras Janice Ascari, Ana Lucia Amaral, Luiza Cristina Frischeisen prosecuted. PGR: Claudio Fonteles.

    Institutional insights

    Internal control failure

    • 1999: A desembargador was recorded selling a sentence. In a closed meeting, the TRF3 president and corregedor-geral decided to destroy the recording in exchange for the judge's early retirement.
    • The corregedoria system only works for first-instance judges. The corregedor cannot investigate desembargadores (rule as of 2001).
    • Desembargadores can only be judged by the STJ (foro privilegiado), which prosecutors used strategically to exclude them from the Anaconda denuncia at TRF3.
    • Administrative processes routinely prescribed (expired). RM's 1992 afastamento reversed in 1996 because the administrative case prescribed.

    Corporativismo (corporate solidarity)

    • In the Haddad indenizacao against the Folha, the judge awarded 1,500 salarios minimos as damages, explicitly to deter future reporting about judges: "para que nao acontecesse com outros juizes."
    • Orgao Especial do TRF3 met in secret session to hear Haddad and Costa's "explanations" of the Folha expose.
    • TRF3 president Kallas resisted opening administrative proceedings: "tem que ter elementos para instaurar procedimento."
    • Some desembargadores wanted Anaconda remitted to STJ. One expressed "receio de represalias" (fear of retaliation).
    • Procuradora Amaral: without media coverage, the tribunal would not have ordered RM's arrest.
    • Defense argument: "Apuracao contra magistrado, em nome de interesse publico, faz-se em sigilo para manter a dignidade da justica."
    • RM threatened retaliation through dossies about colleagues' private lives, and about "familiares de magistrados exercendo advocacia."

    Political connections and judicial nominations

    • Scartezzini faction dominated TRF3. FHC appointed Scartezzini to STJ with backing from Governor Mario Covas and economic-policy officials.
    • Di Salvo, days before taking office as desembargador, ruled in favor of Telefonica, a client of Cid Scartezzini (Jorge's brother, ex-ministro STJ), who had lobbied desembargadores for di Salvo's inclusion in the lista triplice.
    • Consuelo Yoshida's appointment supported by Wilma Motta, widow of ex-minister Sergio Motta.

    Key quotes

    • RM on corruption: "A corrupcao no tribunal e muito facil de apurar ... quando o relator .. a parte procura um ou dois, dois escritorios que trabalham aqui nesse esquema e eles, um deles assuma a causa direto, o outro assume por tras, as vezes."
    • RM asking Cesar Herman for a fake loan: "vc tem condicao de me emprestar, no papel, ne, pro dia 23, 24 de dezembro do ano passado, 50 mil reais? ... Olha, e melhor, em vez de 50, um numero muito redondo, 48 mil ta?"
    • Passarelli to an employee, on destroying evidence: "O crime perfeito so existe se voce pensar em tudo. So isso. Eu consigo fazer porque eu penso em tudo."
    • Procuradora Ana Lucia Amaral on media: "A imprensa so valoriza o fato escandaloso no judiciario quando ha notoriedade dos envolvidos."
    • Bellini to RM after acquitting Chiamarelli: "O Serginho ta mandando um beijo." RM: "Pergunta se ele gostou da decisao ... Ele gostou?" Bellini: "Sim, por isso mesmo, ta te mandando um beijo."

    Relevance to the connect project

    • Direct evidence of family members as conduits: wives acting as advogadas, brothers running shell businesses, ex-wives managing illicit proceeds, nieces/daughters leveraging PF positions, cousins providing cover addresses. This maps directly to the connect project's mechanism taxonomy for how relatives channel influence.
    • Institutional context for Brazilian federal courts: the book documents the TRF3 (second instance), but the institutional features --- corregedoria limitations, corporativismo, random assignment manipulation, foro privilegiado --- apply broadly to the federal judiciary including the labor courts studied in the connect project.
    • Random assignment subversion: multiple documented instances of manipulating case distribution (habeas corpus to specific turmas, manual distribution during recesso) illustrate what random assignment is supposed to prevent. Validates the connect project's reliance on random assignment as an identification strategy.
    • Intermediaries and laranjas: the role of front men (Acidonio, Wagner Rocha), offshore structures, and fake loans provides qualitative context for the types of connections that quantitative analysis may detect as anomalous outcomes.
    • Scale: of 8 federal judges in SP, 4 were removed --- a 50% corruption rate in a single court. Useful for motivating the paper's premise that judicial corruption via family connections is not a rare phenomenon.
    • Corporativismo as obstacle to detection: the institutional resistance to investigating judges (secret sessions, prescription of cases, retaliation fears) explains why family-connection effects may persist undetected in the data and why external identification strategies are necessary.
