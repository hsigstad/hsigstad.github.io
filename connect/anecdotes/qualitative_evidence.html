    Qualitative Evidence: Pricing, Quotes, Interviews

    Anecdotal and qualitative material on the role of connections in the Brazilian legal profession.

    Informal pricing of connections (from Breno)

    Anecdotal price table for connected lawyers:

    • Regular case: R$50K
    • Lawyer with influence: R$60–80K
    • Relative of judge: more
    • Relative of desembargador: R$170K

    Key quotes

    • "A good lawyer knows the law, a great lawyer knows the judge"
    • Eliana Calmon (ex-corregedora, on children of judges): "Os meninos aparecem de BMW, de Mercedes-Benz, morando em casas luxuosas"
    • Almeida: "Quando não há possibilidade legal do nepotismo diretamente, ele ocorre de forma cruzada"

    Kafka reference

    • In The Trial there are mentions of having connected lawyers (noted by Gui, WhatsApp Dec 31 2023)

    Qualitative research on established lawyers

    • Study with interviews of 8 established lawyers (Jair, Miguel, André, Flávio, Hélio, Pedro, Adriano, Mariano)
    • Key patterns: family connections open doors; inheritance of escritórios and clientele; partnerships with larger firms essential
    • 52–53% of magistrates report relatives in private legal practice (Sadek 2006)
    • "Pessoas físicas com maior poder econômico normalmente procuram advogados de renome em função de sua influência sobre os juízes"
