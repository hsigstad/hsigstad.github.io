    Operação Sisamnes (STJ)

    Overview

    Named after Sisamnes, the Persian judge skinned alive for taking bribes. The investigation was triggered by the assassination of lawyer Roberto Zampieri in Cuiabá (December 5, 2023) and the subsequent analysis of his phone (9,000+ WhatsApp messages). Now under STF Minister Cristiano Zanin's relatoria.

    Court: STJ (Superior Tribunal de Justiça) Time period: At least 2019–2024 Phases: 9+ phases from November 2024 through June 2025

    How the scheme worked

    Step 1 — Access to draft decisions: Corrupt assessors and chiefs of staff inside STJ minister offices extracted draft decisions before publication. In some cases, the server would create, save, print, and delete the document within minutes.

    Step 2 — Brokering by lobbyists: Andreson Gonçalves and the late Roberto Zampieri received drafts and shared them with interested parties. If favorable, they "sold" it; if unfavorable, they offered to change it — for a price.

    Step 3 — Payment: Cash, gold bars, Patek Philippe watch (~US$75,000), shell company transfers via Florais Transportes (Andreson's firm, flagged by Coaf for R$2.8 billion in suspicious transactions), inclusion of relatives in lucrative contracts.

    Step 4 — Front: Andreson's wife, advogada Mirian Ribeiro, was formally contracted by interested parties, lending legitimacy. Sometimes the contracts were real legal work, sometimes pure fronts.

    Corrupt assessors

    Servidores in 11 of 33 STJ gabinetes were touched by the investigation:

    • Márcio José Toledo Pinto — served in gabinetes of Ministers Isabel Gallotti and Nancy Andrighi. His shell company Marvan received R$4M from Florais over 2.5 years. Created, saved, printed, and deleted draft decisions within minutes. Demitted September 2024
    • Daimler Alberto de Campos — chief of staff to Minister Isabel Gallotti for over a decade. Bank account received R$8.8M (2019–2023). Continued receiving salary up to R$35,500/month while investigated
    • Rodrigo Falcão de Oliveira Andrade — chief of staff to Minister Og Fernandes. Minister made 82 transfers totaling R$900K to Falcão (Og says for delegated personal bills). Police found 3 empty Rolex boxes with R$266K in receipts
    • Waleska Bertolini Vieira Mussalem — gabinete of Minister Moura Ribeiro. On the same day she drafted a decision, Andreson messaged he had just spoken "com a Valesca"

    The lobbyist couple: Andreson Gonçalves and Mirian Ribeiro

    • Andreson used a fraudulently obtained OAB card (annulled since 2020), owned four aircraft, ran Florais Transportes (R$2.8B in suspicious transactions flagged by Coaf). Arrested November 2024, released to house arrest, re-arrested after continuing to operate from home
    • Mirian Ribeiro (wife, lawyer): Of 201 cases at STJ, 47 passed through Minister Moura Ribeiro's gabinete with 72.5% win rate (vs 43.5% elsewhere). In 16 cases, contracted after case assigned to Moura Ribeiro. Handled 13 J&F/JBS cases; in June 2023, JBS transferred R$16.2M to her office

    Family members of judges/ministers

    Direct family involvement in corruption

    • Mauro Thadeu de Moraes (son of TJ-MT desembargador Sebastião de Moraes Filho): inserted into a R$12 million legal services contract as payment for a favorable decision by his father. In messages: "acertar as honrárias"
    • Anna Carolina Noronha (daughter of STJ Minister João Otávio de Noronha): received R$300,000 from Afare I (Fource group fund linked to the scheme) between 2020–2021. Father claims legitimate legal fees
    • Catarina Buzzi (daughter of STJ Minister Marco Buzzi): rented office from Haroldo Filho (key Fource figure), had contacts with Zampieri. An empresário claimed to have paid R$1.1 million in her favor for a case in her father's gabinete. Did not have OAB card at the time
    • Karine Nunes Marques (sister of STF Minister Kássio Nunes Marques): contracted by Haroldo Filho for a Petrópolis brewery case at STJ. Andreson warned: "Haroldo tem que ver que contratar parentes de ministro do Supremo não é certeza de ganhar"
    • Maria Carolina Feitosa de Albuquerque Tarelho (stepdaughter of STF Minister Gilmar Mendes): reportedly contracted for Petrópolis cases
    • Aline Gonçalves de Sousa (wife of TRF-1 desembargador César Jatahy): received R$938,000 from Florais Transportes

    Broader "filhotismo" pattern

    • 2024 investigation: half of STJ ministers have children/relatives advocating in ~1,000 pending cases
    • Relatives called "príncipes" inside STJ
    • 2022 law (signed by Bolsonaro) worsened the problem by allowing legal consultation "verbally or in writing, at the discretion of the lawyer and client, without requiring a formal mandate" — enabling relatives to work invisibly
