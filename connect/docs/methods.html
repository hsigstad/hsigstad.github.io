Empirical Methods

Contents

    Methods

    Causal effect of connected judge assignment

    Identification strategy

    • Exploit random assignment of cases to court units (varas) in Brazilian labor courts
    • Compare outcomes of cases randomly assigned a connected judge vs an unconnected judge
    • Conditional on court x filing year fixed effects (level of randomization)

    Estimand

    • parameter: Effect of being assigned a judge connected to a lawyer on case outcomes
    • interpretation: Causal effect of judicial connection on favoritism (lower bound, since connected lawyers may also benefit from unconnected judges)

    Specification

    • y_i = alpha_{c(i),t(i)} + beta * Connected_i + epsilon_i
    • y: case outcome (value awarded, plaintiff wins, case duration, etc.)
    • Connected: indicator for assignment to connected judge
    • alpha: court x filing year FE
    • Separate regressions for plaintiff-side and defendant-side connections

    Key assumptions

    • Random assignment of cases to court units within court x year cells
    • No manipulation of assignment conditional on fixed effects
    • Stable unit treatment value assumption (SUTVA)

    Inverse probability weighting

    • Cases connected to more judges have higher mechanical probability of treatment
    • Weight each observation by inverse probability of receiving its actual treatment
    • Creates pseudo-population with 50% treatment probability for all observations
    • Robust to equal weighting and restricting to cases connected to one judge

    Robustness

    • Randomization tests: regress treatment on pre-determined case characteristics (joint F-test p=0.56)
    • Second randomization test: does being assigned judge A increase probability of having lawyer connected to A? (no for plaintiffs, marginal for defendants)
    • Exclude large courts, non-random assignments, deviant court units
    • Results robust to alternative connection definitions and weighting schemes

    Descriptive lawyer success (OLS)

    Identification strategy

    • OLS regression of lawyer success measures on connection indicators
    • Controls: quadratic polynomials in age and schooling, gender, municipality FE
    • Not causal — descriptive comparison of connected vs unconnected lawyers

    Estimand

    • parameter: Association between being connected to a judge and lawyer success measures
    • interpretation: Descriptive, captures both selection and causal channels

    Alternative identification strategies explored

    Home vs away court comparison

    • Compare same lawyer's performance in courts with vs without a connected judge
    • Lawyer fixed effects; home/away/assigned-connected-judge indicators
    • One observation at lawyer x case level
    • Proposed by Shaoda Wang et al
    • Results: correct sign for plaintiff lawyers (0.128 log points) but imprecise; wrong sign for defendant lawyers
    • Limitation: cannot control for all case details unlike Wang et al

    Hiring relative in another case

    • Event plot: large increase in hiring of relative of relator between sentenca and acordao
    • Only of relator, not other desembargadores
    • Random judge design: being assigned relator does not significantly increase chances of hiring relative
    • Driven by few cases; not statistically significant in regressions

    Similar cases comparison

    • Match on case characteristics (assunto, valor da causa, firm size, worker occupation, etc.)
    • Results look promising but could still have bias from unobservables
    • Entropy balancing (ebalance) considered as matching approach

    USP cohort connections

    • 3,568 connections via USP law school
    • Results in expected direction but not statistically significant (~1,200 plaintiff obs, ~1,800 defendant obs)
    • Effect only for same cohort, not adjacent cohorts

    Effect of relative becoming desembargador

    • No detectable discontinuity in lawyer success rates
    • RAIS codes many desembargadores as juiz de trabalho
    • Using case data doesn't work since many serve as juiz convocado

    Judge connected to firm's usual lawyer

    • Number of times firm used a given lawyer in past Y years
    • Tried but limited by few observations in randomization unit of judge

    Firm-judge connections

    • Relative as manager, consultor juridico, socio, or lawyer of the firm
    • Promising for socios and managers but too few observations
