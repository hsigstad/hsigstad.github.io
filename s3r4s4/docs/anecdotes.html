What the stories show (relevance for the project)

Reading the nine collected stories together, four points matter for the paper's institutional framing and for the credibility of the identification strategy:

  1. All five fact patterns from §2.3 of the paper appear in the news record. Story 001 (Claro 2026) is a pure fraud / mistaken-identity case in which the defendant could not produce a signed contract. Story 008 (CPFL Piratininga, TJSP, 2019) is a contested-merits case in which the underlying utility bill itself was adjudicated as abusive. The Migalhas 2020 explainer (004) walks through the paid- debt and missing-pre-notification variants. This supports the claim that 6226 is not a single homogeneous cause of action but a bundle of distinct fact patterns, and it motivates the claim-reason heterogeneity analysis in the main paper.

  2. The typical remedy combines an \emph{obrigação de fazer} and a monetary award. Every plaintiff-win story we have (001, 008, and implicitly the jurisprudence cited in 004) resulted in both (a) a court order removing the disputed entry from Serasa/SPC and (b) a moral-damages award (danos morais). This is exactly the structure the paper models and the decomposition the empirical strategy exploits: removal and damages travel together under the same ruling, so we can separate their contributions using the size variation in the damages component.

  3. The private value of a fast removal is first-order. Story 007 (Folha, 2011) documents a case where a TJ-AM court employee forged a judge's digital signature to obtain an immediate removal of her fiancé from SPC in order to qualify for a home mortgage. Regardless of the legal outcome for the forger, the story illustrates two things we build into the policy section: (a) a single active negative entry is binding for real transactions at the scale of a mortgage, and (b) the lawful route through the civil courts is slow enough that rationally informed actors sometimes choose irregular workarounds. We do not cite this anecdote in the main text (it is colorful rather than scientific evidence), but it is consistent with the comparative statics of the delay-cost back-of-envelope.

  4. The legislative margin is politically contested. Stories 005 and 006 (Folha, 2015--2016) cover PL 44/2016 and related state-level proposals that would have restricted the right of the indebted consumer to be removed from the cadastro. The political salience of negative-registry policy in Brazil is itself a reason that an FCRA-style administrative remedy has not emerged: the political economy of bureau governance is unresolved, and the courts are, de facto, the venue where the balance is struck case by case. This is the backdrop for the policy framing in Section~9 of the paper.

Stories 002, 003, and 009 are either procedural, institutional, or off-topic Estadão discovery noise (see "Known issues" below); they do not add to the framing.

Known issues with newsbr discovery

Fixed 2026-04-14 in newsbr

  • Estadão topic pages were hardcoded for connect. Removed from the default search path; topic crawling is now opt-in via the topic_pages= parameter to newsbr.outlets.estadao.search().
  • Estadão RSS title filter was too permissive. A single matching token (e.g. massa) was enough to accept an item, which let a Spanish immigration story through on the judicialização massa consumo query. Now requires ≥ 2 distinct query tokens to match, and excludes URLs under /pulsa/, /nutricao/, /web-stories/.

Still open

  • Folha first-call hangs on KDE Wallet. Resolved per-session by approving the wallet prompt. make_chrome_session should add a hard timeout so future hangs surface as an error rather than waiting forever.
  • Conjur is fetch-only (Cloudflare blocks search). For serasa we'd need a hand-curated URL list at references/news/curated_conjur.txt if we want Conjur coverage.
  • Estadão RSS coverage is shallow — only the most recent ~25 articles. For credit-registry topics the RSS rarely has anything relevant. Better to use Folha's native search for historical depth, or hand-curate Estadão URLs.

Next collection ideas

  • Pass project-specific Estadão topic pages via newsbr.outlets.estadao.search(query, topic_pages=[...]) — useful candidates: /tudo-sobre/serasa/, /tudo-sobre/dano-moral/, /tudo-sobre/protecao-credito/ (existence to be verified)
  • Hand-curate Conjur URLs on negativação indevida case law
  • Use Folha with --max-pages 3 for more historical depth on the strongest Q1/Q3 queries
  • Add JOTA (legal news, free) as a new outlet — likely strong for this topic
