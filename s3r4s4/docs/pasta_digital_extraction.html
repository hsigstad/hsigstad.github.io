    Extracting data from full case files (pasta digital)

    Two sample pasta digital PDFs downloaded from TJSP e-SAJ, stored in the project root. Not git-tracked. Eventually move to Dropbox.

    Potential source: legal tech firm, ~R$1/process.

    What we already have (consulta processual + sentença pipeline)

    From the TJSP consulta scrape and our sentença classifier we already extract:

    • Party names and roles (author/defendant)
    • valor_da_acao (claim amount set by the lawyer — not the actual debt)
    • Foro, vara, judge name
    • Filing date (data_distribuicao)
    • Case class and subject code (assunto 6226)
    • Movimentações (procedural events with dates)
    • Sentence outcome (procedente / improcedente / parcialmente procedente / extinto / acordo)
    • Defendant type (bank, telecom, retail — from regex on party names)

    What the pasta digital adds (not available from APIs)

    Party identifiers (CPF/CNPJ)

    Field Source within PDF Notes
    Plaintiff CPF Termo de ajuizamento, petição inicial Critical for linking to BCB credit registry, firm registry, employment records. Currently we only have names from consulta.
    Plaintiff CNPJ (when applicable) Bank records, petição inicial PDF 1 reveals the plaintiff's micro-empresa CNPJ — the debt was on a PJ account, invisible from case metadata.
    Defendant CNPJ Filing form, contestação Useful for disambiguating subsidiaries, securitizadoras, debt buyers.

    Debt characterization

    Field Source within PDF Notes
    Actual debt amount Contestação debt table, bank records Distinct from valor_da_acao. The contestação often has a structured table with the updated debt value.
    Product type Contestação, petição inicial E.g., "LIS PJ Pré Aprovado" (overdraft), telecom installments, credit card. Lets us distinguish secured vs. unsecured, consumer vs. business credit.
    Debt origination date Contestação debt table When the debt was first contracted — combined with filing date, gives the age of the debt at lawsuit.
    Contract number Contestação, bank records Could potentially link to BCB credit registry if format matches.
    Whether debt is PJ or PF Bank records, petição inicial PDF 1 reveals the debt was on a micro-empresa account — invisible from case metadata which lists the individual plaintiff.

    Credit market / secondary debt market

    Field Source Notes
    Cessão (debt assignment) details Cessão declarations, contestação Who bought the debt (securitizadora), when, from whom. Maps the secondary debt market.
    Collection agent Contestação, bank response E.g., Recovery do Brasil handling collection for iResolve.
    Serasa Limpa Nome listing details Screenshots in exhibits What the consumer actually sees: listed amount, discount offers, payment plans. Direct evidence of the platform's role.

    Plaintiff characteristics (beyond party name)

    Field Source Notes
    Demographics Termo de ajuizamento, RG copy Age (from birth date), gender, race, marital status, occupation. Much richer than what consulta gives.
    Employment status Petição inicial E.g., "desempregada", "autônomo". Relevant for understanding who litigates.
    Justiça gratuita Petição inicial Whether plaintiff claims inability to pay court costs — proxy for income.
    Address Filing form Municipality, neighborhood. Could geo-link.

    Pre-litigation and procedural detail

    Field Source Notes
    PROCON complaint Attached CIP documents Whether consumer went through PROCON before court, and what happened.
    Conciliation details CEJUSC terms Whether conciliation was attempted, attendance, offers made. Goes beyond the movimentação code.
    Moral damages amount requested Petição inicial Specific R$ amount or formula (e.g., "40 salários mínimos").
    Moral damages amount awarded Sentença text When granted, the exact amount — not just win/lose.

    Litigation industry

    Field Source Notes
    Law firm identity Letterhead, procurações Identify high-volume template-litigation firms (e.g., De Nicola Advogados in PDF 2 — 80-page template petition, multiple office locations).
    Template vs. bespoke petition Petição inicial Length, boilerplate ratio, jurisprudence dump style. Indicator of industrialized litigation.
    Legal arguments used Petição inicial, contestação LGPD angle, prescription doctrine, exercício regular de direito. Could track argument diffusion over time.

    Bank/creditor internal records

    Field Source Notes
    Ficha de cobrança Defendant exhibits Full arrears timeline from the bank's internal system: dates of each missed payment, charges, interest. Only available here.
    Bank response to PROCON Email exhibits How the bank characterizes the debt internally.

    What's NOT worth extracting (already covered or low value)

    • Procedural certifications and publication dates — already in movimentações
    • Powers of attorney chain — unless studying lawyer networks
    • Corporate identity documents of defendants (AGE minutes etc.)
    • Boilerplate legal text — jurisprudence citations in briefs are voluminous but low signal for our RD

    Feasibility

    • At R$1/process and ~300K cases in our Datajud sample, full download would cost ~R$300K — need to sample strategically
    • Could target: (a) cases with sentences only, (b) specific varas/judges, (c) specific defendant types
    • Text quality is mixed: some pages are clean digital text, others are scanned/OCR. LLM extraction would be needed for many fields.
    • The most unique data (ficha de cobrança, cessão details, demographics) only appears in a subset of cases — not every PDF will have bank internal records.
