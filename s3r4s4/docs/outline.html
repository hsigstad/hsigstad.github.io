Paper Outline

Living skeleton for the paper "Redress Delayed, Business Denied: Credit Blacklisting and Entrepreneurship in Brazil". Section purposes and dependencies only — not drafted prose. Update when scope or structure changes; draft prose belongs in paper/paper.tex.

Argument structure

  • Claim: wrongful inclusion in Brazilian credit bureaus (Serasa) is frequent, courts correct a large share of wrongful listings, and judicial correction causally improves firm survival and entrepreneurial activity. Judicial delay therefore imposes real costs on small firms.
  • Evidence: (i) descriptive: ~80% of ~118k Sao Paulo rulings on "inclusao indevida" favor the plaintiff, (ii) IV using vara-level leniency on TJSP cases, (iii) outcomes from CNPJ/Receita (firm activity, plants, MEI, Simples) and BCB credit registry (credit stock, pricing, guarantees, prejuizo).
  • Contribution: first microeconomic evidence on the interaction of wrongful credit reporting and weak judicial redress; fills a gap in the credit-bureau / consumer-protection literature that has been silent on the absence-of-protection margin.
