Research Summary

Summary

Research question

  • How do court decisions in credit registry disputes affect entrepreneurship and firm outcomes in Brazil?

Motivation

  • Improper inclusion in credit registries (Serasa) is a major source of litigation in Brazilian courts
  • Court outcomes in these cases can directly affect individuals' and firms' access to credit
  • Judicial delay and unpredictable outcomes may discourage entrepreneurship

Design

  • Use TJSP court data on "Inclusao Indevida em Cadastro de Inadimplentes" cases (assunto code 6226)
  • Two court data sources: Datajud API (case metadata) and TJSP consulta (full case detail with parties, movements)
  • Classify litigants as banks, firms, or individuals using regex patterns on court text
  • Extract sentence outcomes (procedente, improcedente, parcialmente procedente, extinto, acordo)
  • Instrument for court outcomes using leave-one-out judge leniency (by foro-vara-year)
  • Link court data with Central Bank administrative records (credit registry, firm registry, employment)

Data partnership

  • Central Bank of Brazil provides individual-level administrative data
  • Leonardo Alencar (Senior Advisor, Central Bank) facilitates data access
