Spec: Vara-level instrument construction

Vara-level instrument construction

Status: draft Priority: high Requested by: Henrik For: All IV regressions (paper §4–6); first-stage table; balance table

What to change

The current instrument bias_magistrado is a leave-one-out judge-level win rate: for each case, the mean of won_case across all other cases decided by the same magistrado. This is conceptually wrong because the randomization unit is the vara, not the judge. Cases are randomly assigned to varas by the sorteio eletrônico; the judge is whoever happens to occupy the vara at that time.

Option A: pure vara leniency (preferred starting point)

Replace bias_magistrado with bias_vara: the leave-one-out win rate of the vara in the same foro-year cell.

In Stata, this means replacing:

* Current (judge-level):
bys magistrado: egen aux = sum(won_case)
bys magistrado: egen aux2 = count(won_case)
gen bias_magistrado = (aux - won_case) / (aux2 - 1)

with:

* Proposed (vara-level, within foro × year):
bys vara comarca_id year_processo: egen aux = sum(won_case)
bys vara comarca_id year_processo: egen aux2 = count(won_case)
gen bias_vara = (aux - won_case) / (aux2 - 1)

Then use bias_vara in place of bias_magistrado in all RF and IV regressions. The FE structure stays the same (cnpj_basico, comarca_id#year_processo, year_processo#year).

Option B: weighted judge average (more powerful, second pass)

For varas that have seen multiple judges over time, construct vara leniency as a weighted average of the leniencies of all judges who have served in the vara, weighted by each judge's total decision count (possibly across all case types, not just 6226). This leverages each judge's full decision history for leniency estimation.

This is more complex and should be done after Option A is working.

Clustering

Also change clustering from vce(cluster cnpj_basico) to vce(cluster vara) in all IV regressions, since the source of identifying variation is at the vara level. Report both for comparison.

Rationale

The paper's identification argument is that cases are randomly assigned to varas within foros. The instrument should match the randomization unit. Araújo et al. (2023, JFE) use the same vara-level construction for TJSP bankruptcy cases.

Expected output

Same table/figure format as the 14042026 batch, with:

  • bias_vara replacing bias_magistrado
  • Both vce(cluster cnpj_basico) and vce(cluster vara) versions
  • First-stage F-statistic for the new instrument
  • Please also report: number of unique varas in the sample, mean/sd of bias_vara within foro-year, and the number of cases per vara-year cell (p10, p50, p90)

Name the output folder with the date and suffix -vara, e.g., 20042026-vara/.
